At Willow Café, everything we serve — from our signature drinks to our welcoming atmosphere — is the result of a shared vision by five passionate individuals who believe in building more than just a café. Get to know the people behind the brew:



Tee Yu Qing — The Strategist

Co-Founder & Business Manager

Yu Qing handles the numbers so the rest of the team can focus on the experience. With years in F&B operations and finance, she oversees daily management, supplier relations, and long-term planning.

Ding Yee Qing — The Flavor Curatorg

Co-Founder & Head of Kitchen

With a background in culinary arts, Yee Qing designs a menu that's as diverse as it is delicious. Her goal? To make sure everyone finds something they love — whether it's a savory breakfast wrap or a decadent slice of cake.

Lim Shan En — The Coffee Artisan

Co-Founder & Head of Beverages

Shan En spent years perfecting the art of coffee, from bean sourcing to espresso pulling. A certified barista and coffee competitor, she leads the beverage team and ensures every cup served at Willow meets her high standards.

Sin Shen Nee — The Visionary

Co-Founder & Creative Director

Shen Nee dreamed up Willow Café as a space where comfort and creativity meet. With a background in interior design and a deep love for slow mornings, she shaped the café's cozy, modern aesthetic and community-first philosophy.

Lee Hui Wen — The Storyteller

Co-Founder & Marketing Lead

Hui Wen brings Willow Café to life online and offline. From social media to seasonal campaigns, she tells the café's story with heart, helping Willow connect with the community and beyond.
